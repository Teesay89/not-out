How it works

  • Press the ball. Every press deals you a random nation and a random decade — 1970s West Indies, 1990s Pakistan, 2020s India…
  • Pick from the whole squad. Every available player from that nation's decade comes up. Choose one, then choose where he goes in your batting order.
  • Every player is rated for his own decade — how he ranked among his contemporaries. A 97 from the ’80s is every bit as good as a 97 from today.
  • Fill the card your way. Openers up top, keeper at 6, flex at 7, bowlers at the death — but you choose the order you fill them. Players with no position left for them (say, a keeper when yours is picked) simply won't appear. And you still get one do-over per game for a hopeless draw.
  • Beat them all, at their place. Your XI tours the world: five matches against each of today’s twelve international sides, all sixty played in their stadiums. The dream: win the lot. Every match is played to a finish — no rain, no escapes.

Fair warning: the draw doesn't care about your plans. Sometimes you need a spinner and it deals you the 1984 West Indies pace battery.
