Classic Mode

How it works

  • Press the ball. Every press deals you a random nation and a random decade — 1890s England, 1970s West Indies, 2020s India…
  • Choose your era first. Test lets you pick how far back to reach — Full Archive for the whole story, Vintage XI for the 1940s onward, Colour TV Era for the 1970s onward, or Hawk-Eye Era for the 2000s onward. ODI keeps it simple: Whites for everything, or Coloured Kit Era for the 1990s onward. T20's whole history already fits in one era, so there's nothing to choose.
  • Pick from the whole squad. Every available player from that nation's decade comes up. Choose one, then choose where he goes in your batting order.
  • Every player is rated for his own decade — how he ranked among his contemporaries. A 97 from the ’80s is every bit as good as a 97 from today.
  • Fill the card your way. Openers up top, bowlers at the death — but there's no fixed slot for the keeper any more, he just needs to be somewhere in your side. All-rounders slot in based on how they actually played: some open, some finish. Players with no legal spot left simply won't appear. And you still get one do-over per game for a hopeless draw.
  • Set your lineup, then pick a captain. Before the tour starts, fine-tune your order — tap or drag to swap anyone into a different spot — then name your captain. Players batting in their correct position give the team a small boost, and so does a captain with a genuine, real-world winning record for that side.
  • Beat them all, at their place. Your XI tours the world: a five-match series against each of today’s eleven international sides, played across real grounds in their country, before a five-match finale at Lord's against a randomly-drawn World All-Star XI — sixty matches in total. Win matches, win series, sweep one 5-0 — that's what separates a good tour from a legendary one.

Fair warning: the draw doesn't care about your plans. Sometimes you need a spinner and it deals you the 1984 West Indies pace battery.

How It Works

Position

Every player has a natural home in the batting order. Put him there and he's IN POSITION — anywhere else and he's OUT OF POSITION, which costs a little off his contribution. Correctly-placed players who make your best 7 batters or best 5 bowlers also add a small individual lift to your team.

Captaincy

Some players in this game were genuinely outstanding captains — real winning records, for that exact country, format and decade. Naming one of them your captain (look for the 🎖️ ELITE CAPTAIN tag) gives your team a small boost too.

Rearranging

You can fine-tune your order any time before the tour starts — tap the ⇅ handle to pick a swap, or press and drag a row straight onto another.

Scoring

Once the tour is under way, your team's overall strength is measured against each opponent to decide who wins. Your final score rewards winning matches, winning series, whitewashing a series 5-0, and winning convincingly rather than just scraping through — so a genuinely dominant, well-built XI scores far better than one that just gets over the line.
